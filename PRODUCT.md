# BongoFoodie product context

A premium Swahili food delivery app for Dar es Salaam, designed both as a polished showcase and a practical product. The experience should feel warm, appetising and confident rather than corporate. Customers discover dishes, explore Chef AI, order food and follow delivery; restaurant staff use the corresponding operational tools. Support Kiswahili and English, accessible interaction, and a consistent visual system across mobile and web.
